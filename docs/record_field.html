Record field

The wheel of the gears 5 … y on the m-line (column k is the pair (12k − 1, 12k + 1); gear h paints k ≡ ∓12⊃−1⊃ mod h) at its worst phase: the longest painted run over a full period, the record Ry. Rows are the gears, columns the k of the run with a margin either side. Yellow: h divides the left member; red: the right member. The all row is black where painted, bright green where open. The record run is boxed in purple. Each record run has a mirror image about half the period; the run shown is the first.
