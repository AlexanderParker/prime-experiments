The paint just above the top gear q. Columns are the natural numbers q + d. Row h is painted where h divides q + d (one class of d per row, phase fixed by q). Twin slots (numbers 1 or 5 mod 6) show the kill colour, yellow left member, red right member; other numbers white. The all row: bright green = a twin prime pair, dark green = an aligned twin (midpoint a multiple of 12, the walk’s landing family), dark blue = a prime, black = composite. The purple tick marks the walk’s landing, the first aligned twin above q. Rows are drawn up to the largest gear that can reach the shown range (5h ≤ q + N); rows above it paint nothing here.
