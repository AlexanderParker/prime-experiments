Mirror walk

Nodes are columns (n, n+2). Home is (−1, 1). A flip about an axis k·M/2, M the product of a gear subset containing 2 and 3, sends n to k·M − n − 2 and carries every gear dividing the axis product k·M (more than the axis family names, sometimes). Gear pairs (both members gears of the machine) are marked orange: known twins, struck only by themselves. Use the pair buttons to flip the current node straight onto a gear pair; the axis is then (n + g + 2)/2 and the carried gears are those dividing n + g + 2. Click a column to stand on it; click an axis label to flip about it. The window (q, q²] is boxed in purple, the mirror stretches of the window (near k·q#) in yellow.
