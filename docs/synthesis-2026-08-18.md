## I. One gear
Per six rotations: one left-kill and one right-kill bracketing a shield, three misses. Slot space:
teeth at +-u summing to q (mirror), u = round(q/6) = the gear's own pair (self-blocking), shield
at 0 centred in the short umbrella, umbrellas 1/3 + 2/3 and growing, kill density 2/q -> 0. One
symmetry (the pair structure mirrored at the shield) yields the teeth sum, centred shield,
symmetric pattern, and half-period axis.

## II. Composition preserves the laws
Any subset is a composite gear: +-1 walk, family by sign product, self-blocks its product's pair,
teeth summing to its modulus (verified through triples). The only new object is the crossed cloud:
2^n - 2 mixed lifts, mirror-paired with complementary signatures, placed by slip. The machine is
self-similar at all 2^n scales; what is not self-similar is exactly the cloud's placement.

## III. What kills, where
Each gear's root territory opens at its own square (shadowed below; square, then coprimes).
Territories collapse upward (9, 6, 2, 0 in the 13-window); the top gear's is beyond the horizon
(proved; verified y = 13..79). Overlaps = crossed double-kills. Deciding acts = pseudo-twins
(kills beside a prime, fragile slots); the low gears are near-100% deciding. Half of all blocking
is direct semiprime re-entry of the machine's own output; at the twin-deciding margin,
exclusively so.

## IV. Sufficiency
The whole window needs essentially every gear (square gate: q^2 - 2 primality sets permanent
floors at 5, 7, 13, 19, 29, 37, 43, 47...). Finding the first twin needs only sqrt-depth (6 of 75
gears at y = 389). Downward exclusion = nested horizons + partner luck, bottoming at the first
pseudo-twin owner. One layer of the advancing tower adds an explicit short list: the retiring
square plus one to three y*c semiprimes.

## V. The tree
Classes branch q-2 per gear (turn law), never extinct, prune provably sound - the constructor
tree-shaped, generating every twin in-window at every scale run. Twins are where the diagonal
(all remainders in lockstep) hits the all-umbrella set.

## VI. The one open quantity, in every costume
The sideways step when a branch dies = the stride of the state walk (single-point-failure chains
anchored on coincidence hubs) = stride growth under a new gear (exact chain arithmetic; fuel words
rare or absent; k = 4 first sighted at y = 29; fuel abundance plausibly explains the gear-37
anomaly) = the kappa margin (limit 0.5448 at L = 2; proven positive for L <= 3 at every y) = the
growth law of F (F(2,53) >= 420 standing; diagnostics lean geometric). All one number: whether the
all-umbrella slot always recurs inside the window - Reduction A, machine-checked equivalent to the
twin prime conjecture.
