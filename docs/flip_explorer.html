Flip explorer

Rows are gears, columns the natural numbers (yellow: the gear divides a left member, n ≡ 5 mod 6; red: a right member; grey: a number outside the twin slots). Tick the gears to include in the mirror; 2 and 3 are always in. The axis sits relative to the origin pair: k periods of the included gears away from it, at the origin’s centre plus k·M (M the product of the included gears). Flipping the origin about that axis lands 2k·M above the origin: the included gears see the same residues there (their pattern repeats every M), so their openness is carried; every other gear is left to chance. Click a column to stand on it (the origin), then Flip: the origin’s mirror image about the axis is the landing, the reflected stretch between them is shaded, and the table shows for every gear whether the flip carried its openness (the gear divides 2kM) or left it to chance. The window (q, q²] is boxed in purple; home (−1, 1) is the column at −1.
