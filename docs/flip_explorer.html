Flip explorer

A walk built step by step. Each step is one flip: tick the gears in its mirror (2 and 3 always in), set how many periods k of those gears the axis sits from the origin (the axis is the origin’s centre plus k·M), and the landing is 2k·M above the origin. The next step takes that landing as its origin. Rows are gears, columns the natural numbers; yellow a struck left member, red a struck right member, grey outside the twin slots. The included gears see the same residues at the landing (their pattern repeats every M), so their openness is carried; the table says which gears the step carried and which it left to chance. The window (q, q²] is boxed purple.
