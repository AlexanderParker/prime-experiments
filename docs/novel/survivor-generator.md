## 1. WHAT IT IS

Plain language. Adding a gear `q'` to a sieve machine `M` deletes some of its
openings; the surviving openings are the openings of `M + q'`, and the new gaps
are sums of runs of old gaps. R46 (`kleene-generator.md`) showed that the
LARGEST new gap is a max-plus Kleene star over the old machine:
`F(M + q') = L (x) K* (x) R`, where the states are (killed opening, tooth), `K`
carries the legal spacings between consecutive killed openings, and `L`, `R`
are the two flanking old gaps. That identity is arity-free - it never names a
depth - but it computes only ONE number, the maximal new gap.

This document extends it to the whole low spectrum of the new machine by adding
exactly one transition.

**THEOREM (SURVIVOR IDENTITY).** Let `M` be a machine, `q'` the next prime,
`c = 6^{-1} mod q'`, `u' = round(q'/6)`, letters `a = 2u'`, `b = q' - 2u'`. Let
`d_i` be the gaps of `M` and `cls(v)` the class of a spacing `v` mod `q'`
(`0`, `a`, `b`, or none). Define the SKIP transition

    SIGMA[(i,s),(i+2,s')] = d_i + d_{i+1}
        whenever cls(d_i)          is ILLEGAL out of tooth s   (so the opening
                                    i+1 SURVIVES gear q'), and
                 cls(d_i + d_{i+1}) is LEGAL out of tooth s with landing tooth s'.

Then

        F_2(M + q')  =  L (x) K* (x) SIGMA (x) K* (x) R ,

and more generally `F_j(M + q') = L (x) K* (x) (SIGMA (x) K*)^{j-1} (x) R`
(the proof below covers every j; the script verifies j = 2 only - the deeper
layers are NOT yet script-checked).

Proof. A window of `j` consecutive gaps of `M + q'` is exactly a window of
consecutive openings of `M` in which every interior opening is killed by `q'`
except `j-1`, which survive. All killed openings in the window sit at the SAME
`q'`-phase, so by the two-teeth kill-spacing law (`two-teeth-kill-spacing.md`,
T2/T3) their consecutive spacings lie in `{0, a, b}` mod `q'` with strictly
alternating nonzero signs - that is the transition relation of `K`. Between the
last killed opening of one run and the first of the next there is EXACTLY ONE
old opening (any other opening there would itself be killed and would belong to
a run, contradicting maximality of the runs), so the straddling spacing is
`d_i + d_{i+1}` and is itself a `K`-legal spacing; and the intermediate opening
`i+1` survives precisely when `d_i` alone is NOT a legal transition out of the
current tooth, since `x_k = ±c` forces `x_k + d_i = ±c` exactly when
`d_i in {0, ∓2c}`. Conversely any word accepted by the expression is realised
by choosing the CRT copy of `q'` that kills the required openings. Endpoint
relaxation: the two ends of the window may be taken to be arbitrary openings
rather than survivors, because enlarging a window with `j-1` interior survivors
only enlarges the enclosing `j`-gap window of `M + q'`, so the maximum is
unchanged. QED

Boundary cases are the two degenerate branches: the left run empty (the window
opens with a single unmerged old gap) and the right run empty (`SIGMA` then
stop). Both are written out explicitly in the script.

**COROLLARY (the two-gap statement is layer 0, one gear down).** Requirement
(D)'s layer 0 at machine `M + q'` - the two-gap statement
`F_2(M + q') <= F(M + q') + q''` - is a Kleene statement over `M`. So the
m-point history abstraction `A_m` of R49, whose entire machine input is the
dictionary of REALISED gap m-tuples of `M`, bounds it. In particular the
realised-PAIR sub-dictionary of `A_m` IS `F_2(M)`: the "extra integer" a
certificate at step `M -> M + q'` needs is not an extra hypothesis, it is a
projection of the dictionary that same certificate is already querying.

## 2. WHY IT MIGHT BE NOVEL

The one-gear merge law (`merge-law.md`) and its Kleene form (R46) already say
that `F(M+q')` is computable from `M`'s gap word alone. What is new here:

* the whole LOW SPECTRUM `F_2, F_3, ...` of the new machine, not just its
  maximal gap, is generated by the SAME finite max-plus automaton with one
  extra letter - the automaton does not grow with `j`, only the word does;
* the extra letter has an exact arithmetic characterisation - "the spacing
  qualifies but its first half does not" - which is a two-teeth statement with
  no analogue in the one-residue (ordinary Jacobsthal) setting, where every
  spacing has a single class;
* it converts a hypothesis into a projection: the layer-0 input of a
  certificate at one step is an output of the certificate at the previous step,
  which is what closes an induction that otherwise needs one period scan per
  rung.

Classical shadows to check: transfer-matrix / automaton descriptions of gap
statistics in wheel sieves; Holt-Rudd's cycle recursion for Jacobsthal
(one class per prime, so no SIGMA analogue); max-plus automata for
longest-path problems in general.

The `F(M+q')` column is asserted against the known ladder (11, 18, 25, 34, 43,
58) and the `F_2(M+q')` column against the independent full-period lag-1 pair
census of Mechanic (16, 25, 31, 39, 55, 68) - two independent computations of
the same six integers, agreeing exactly. The machine-29 pass is 1,078,282,205
slots / 214,709,355 gaps in 125 s.

The `A_4` column is the sound history abstraction: states are realised gap
3-tuples of `M`, ordinary edges are realised 4-tuples, and SKIP transitions are
COMPOSED from two realised 4-tuples (so no 5-tuple fact is ever used). It is
exact at the first three steps and loose by +3, +2, +25 at the last three; at
every step it clears the next step's two-gap budget `F(M+q') + q''`
(margins +12, +12, +17, +21, +17, +2).

`A_5` (realised 5-tuples) RESTORES EXACTNESS at all three steps where `A_4`
is loose: 39, 55, 68 = the exact `F_2` values at 19->23, 23->29, 29->31
(the last from 91,708 states / 14,779 + 20,943 edges, log
`research/data/survivor_29_m5.log`). So the survivor system needs exactly ONE
MORE order of history than the plain system (whose `A_4` is exact 7/7, R49) -
and `A_5(23)` delivers the literal round-23 integer `F_2(29) = 55` with no
machine-29 scan.
