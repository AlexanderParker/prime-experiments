Final Step Positions

The final step of the locator, drawn as the fields construction: rows are the machine's gears, columns are the high gears h in reach. The primorial spiral lands at E; the step about {3, h} lands at E ± 6h. A mark in row g, column h is one kill of one field type: the letter is the member struck (L = left, R = right), the digit is the order of the composite, so L2 is the field higher:g of order 2 (g times a prime) on the left member, R3 is order 3 on the right, and so on. A gear whose E ≡ −1 has its teeth fixed on column h itself; every other gear's teeth are placed by E. A column with no mark is a passing gear: the landing is a twin prime pair.
