Locator field

The locator seen as a field. Columns are the natural numbers m; the candidate is the column at offset i after m², the pair (m² + 6i − 2, m² + 6i). Row h is painted where gear h strikes that candidate: yellow when h divides the left member, red the right, white when the candidate is not a twin slot at all (m not coprime to 6). Rows are periodic in m with period h; the painted classes are the square roots of −(6i − 2) and −6i mod h; a row with no paint is a blind gear for this offset. The all row: bright green = m is prime and its candidate is a twin (a hit gear), dark green = m prime, candidate open to every gear shown but not a twin (a larger gear strikes), dark blue = m prime and struck, black = m composite. The machine box marks m in (√q, q], the gears whose square lies in machine q’s window.
