The Fields

Each row is one field on the fold's survivors, drawn column by column. A column is the slot (6k − 1, 6k + 1). A cell is painted when the field contains the column's left member (upper half), right member (lower half), or both. Hover a cell for the members and their factorisations.

Definitions: field j = members with exactly j prime factors counted with multiplicity (all ≥ 5). Gear field of g = members whose least prime factor is g (g times a survivor of the gears below g, from g² on). Strictly-higher field of g = the gear field of g with g dividing exactly once. Smooth field of g = composites whose prime factors all lie in {5, …, g}; its layer = those whose largest factor is g. Overlay = every composite member. Source: research/proof/fields_construction.md.
