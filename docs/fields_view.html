The Fields

Every field is a grid: one row per gear (the primes up to the chosen top gear), one column per natural number. A cell is painted when that gear takes part in the field's strike at that number: green or blue when the number is prime, yellow when the hit kills a left member (n ≡ 5 mod 6), red when it kills a right member (n ≡ 1 mod 6), white when it kills nothing. A column no gear of the field strikes is shaded down its whole height: dark green if the number is a twin prime member, dark blue if a standalone prime, black if neither. The last row of every field, marked "all", is the combined view: the field's strikes in their colours, and every other column in its open colour. Hover a cell for the factorisation. The reference strip at the top marks every prime.
