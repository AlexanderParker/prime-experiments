(1) THE THEOREM
---------------
Let M be a machine, q' = nextprime(y), u = 6^{-1} mod q' taken as the SMALL
representative, d' = 2u, a = d', b = q' - a, a_min = min(a,b), G = F(M + q').

  (i)   the smallest positive gap value in class +d' is a, in class -d' is b,
        in the padded class 0 mod q' it is q'          [gated, 12 machines]
  (ii)  T3 makes the nonzero-class letters strictly alternate, so any two
        CONSECUTIVE nonzero letters of a legal word sum to >= a + b = q'
  (iii) a realised legal word of m letters is the middle of a window
        x_0 < ... < x_{m+2} of consecutive openings of M whose m middle gaps
        are legal, so R68's ATTAINMENT THEOREM (PROVED - Constructor R68/R46:
        "if consecutive openings x_0..x_J of M have a legal middle-gap word
        then x_J - x_0 <= F(M+q')") gives span + before + after <= G, and
        before, after >= 1 (distinct openings).

With p padded letters, n = m - p nonzero ones,
    span >= p q' + floor(n/2) q' + [n odd] a_min,
so with T = floor((G - 2)/q'):

    (SIMPLE)  L(M) <= 2T + 1,     and sharper L(M) <= 2T + 1 - p
    (PARITY)  L(M) <= max( 2T, 2*floor((G - 2 - a_min)/q') + 1 )

    i.e.  L(M) <= 2 F(M+q')/q' + 1.

STATUS: UNCONDITIONAL given R68 (proved) and T3 (R89's word reduction).
L is O(F/q'), not O(1).

(3) THE FAMILY: IS L JUST THE SIZE OF THE LETTERS?  NO.
-------------------------------------------------------
Round 30's chunk data, 165,584 rows over six steps (19->23 full 142,560;
23->29 a 601-member sample).  G taken direct where round 30 has it, else the
record law max(F_2, max_J Q*_J) (record law == direct at every direct row,
re-gated here).

GATE: (SIMPLE), (PARITY) and the padded-aware L <= 2T+1-p hold at EVERY ONE of
the 165,584 rows - 0 violations, including the family's L = 5 member, where
PARITY = 5 exactly.  The theorem is not a corpus coincidence.

L IS NOT GOVERNED BY a_min.  spearman(L, a_min) = -0.277, +0.006, +0.065,
+0.088, -0.159, -0.245 across the six steps - never below -0.28, POSITIVE at
three steps.  a_min = min(2v, q'-2v) is a BIJECTIVE relabelling of v_q' on
1..(q'-1)/2, so eta^2(L | a_min) = eta^2(L | v_q') exactly (ratio 1.000 at all
six steps): a_min carries the incoming tooth's information, no more and no
less, but the ORDER of L in a_min is not monotone.

        P(L>=3):  0.0000 0.3008 0.2478 0.0002 0.5823 0.0005 0.1552 0.0464
                  0.0000 0.1275 0.0381
THE SMALLEST LETTER IS THE WORST: a_min = 1 (letters 22, 1) has max L = 2 and
P(L >= 3) = 0 over 12,960 rows, while a_min = 5 (letters 18, 5) reaches L = 5
with P(L >= 3) = 0.58.  "Small letters = long imitations" is REFUTED.

WHY (mechanism, why_amin.log): the binding quantity is the SUPPLY of the rarer
letter class, and it is the small letter's PARTNER that is rare.  At 19->23 the
mean min(n_a, n_b) over 378,675 gaps runs 42, 48, 164, 142, 267, 223, 314,
1131, 1956, 2977, 1452 as a_min goes 1..11 - spearman(a_min, min(n_a,n_b)) =
+0.816.  But L does NOT track supply either: spearman(L, min(n_a,n_b)) =
+0.081.  The best single correlate found is the PADDED-letter supply,
spearman(L, n_0) = +0.311.

REAL MACHINE inside the family: a_min/q' = 0.364, 0.308, 0.353, 0.316, 0.348,
0.345 - always near 1/3, never extreme; 50-64% of family rows have a SMALLER
a_min than the real machine and their mean L is at or above the real value at
every step.  So the real machine's letters are ordinary in size; what is not
ordinary is the corridor.

VERDICT on the framing: the "arithmetic of the teeth" in (B) is NOT the size of
the letters.  It is one line only in the sense that the SIZE half is now a
theorem (the spectrum bound); the residue half is not, and the two are
orthogonal on the family.
