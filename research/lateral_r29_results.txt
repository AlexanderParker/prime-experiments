BLOCK B - THE REVERSAL SYMMETRY AT EVEN J
================================================================================
THE MAP.  R_J(t) = -(t+J) on window indices (mod N); on addresses
k -> -(k + span); on words, REVERSAL; on killing residues r -> -(r + span).
It preserves depth, span, interior count, T2 (value-wise) and T3 (alternation
is reversal-invariant), so the word-legal family is R_J-invariant.  N odd gives
exactly one fixed point per depth: the SELF-MIRROR window, centred on slot 0
(J even) or on the antipode (J odd).

B.1 THEOREM (new).  For every J >= 3 the self-mirror depth-J window is NEVER
    WORD-LEGAL.
      J ODD: its central middle is the gap straddling the antipode, of length 1
        (both antipodal slots are openings - lateral 7.3).  1 is a legal letter
        iff 1 = 0 or +-2u' (mod q'); 2u' = 2*6^{-1} = 3^{-1}, so that needs
        3 = +-1 (mod q'), i.e. q' | 2 or q' | 4.  Impossible for q' >= 5.
      J EVEN >= 4: its two CENTRAL middles are BOTH d_0, the machine's first
        gap.  T3 forbids two equal nonzero classes in a row; 0 < d_0 < q'
        forbids both being padded.
      J = 2: no middles, so the self-mirror 2-window (d_0, d_0) IS legal - the
        one depth where the lever needs a hypothesis, and there it is d_0 != F.
    CONSEQUENCE: R_J is fixed-point-free on the word-legal family at every
    J >= 3, so every span count there is EVEN with NO exceptional class and no
    census.  This upgrades lateral round 26 item 7.5 (a 66-cell check) and
    round 28 item 65 (a span table plus an exception list) to a theorem.

B.3 THE ANSWER TO "DOES EVEN J GIVE A NEW INEQUALITY": NO.
    R_J is span-preserving, so the only object it adds is the quotient of the
    legal family by an involution, whose cardinality is |family|/2.  Every
    consequence is therefore the SAME ONE UNIT the odd-J route gives - "fewer
    than two proves none" instead of "fewer than one" - and no inequality on
    F_J or Q*_J follows.  What IS new is that at even J the unit is FREE: the
    hypothesis is discharged by B.1 rather than by a per-machine check.
    (PROVED for counting arguments over the legal family; JUDGMENT, NOT RESULT
    for "any argument whatsoever".)
